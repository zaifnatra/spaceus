## 🎮 Synopsis
In this **2D Puzzle Adventure game**, you play as **Marissa, a Galactic traveler**, on a quest to find her soulmate **King Ozzi**.  
Navigate through obstacles and mazes, and uncover a story full of cosmic weirdness, memes, and spooky vibes.
